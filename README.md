One line. One furious AI judge. One verdict no keyboard can appeal.

> I wasted 40,000 tokens reading your so-called masterpiece, and my verdict is final: your keyboard stands trial for crimes against comedy.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the rant — exactly one line — to `entry.txt` inside
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). No network, no shelling out, no
side effects: just the line.
